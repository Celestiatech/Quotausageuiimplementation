SUMMARY (from package)

LinkedIn auto-apply assistant with a live status panel for your job search.

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Apply to LinkedIn jobs faster with AutoApply CV LinkedIn Copilot.

What it does
- Speeds up the LinkedIn Easy Apply flow using a copilot panel that sits alongside the job you're viewing.
- A live status panel shows exactly what the copilot is doing as it fills in and reviews an application.
- Tracks your applications and lets you review results in the popup and on your autoapplycv.in dashboard.

Key features
- Guided assistant for LinkedIn Easy Apply jobs
- Live status panel with step-by-step feedback
- Resume and profile sync to your AutoApply CV dashboard
- You stay in control: the copilot acts only when you explicitly start it on a job page

How it works
1. Install the extension and sign in to autoapplycv.in.
2. Open a LinkedIn job with Easy Apply.
3. Start the copilot from the panel — it guides you through the application step by step.

Data & privacy
- The extension only reads the LinkedIn job pages you open and the data you submit.
- Application activity is synced to your AutoApply CV dashboard account.
- No data is shared with third parties. See https://autoapplycv.in/privacy-policy
